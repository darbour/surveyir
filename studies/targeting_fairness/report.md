# Targeting Fairness: end-to-end study report

_Generated by `studies/targeting_fairness/run.py` on 2026-10-08. Do not edit by hand._

**Study.** Twin-2K-500 mega-study replication *Segmentation fairness: gender, taste* (`survey.qsf`). One between-subjects factor, randomizer `FL_491` (1 of 2, evenly presented), sets `Segment`; display logic on `Segment` then shows one of two text vignettes on the same page as the outcome, `fair1` ("How fair is this advertising plan?", 1 = not at all fair, 9 = very fair).

| arm | records `Segment` | records `target` | vignette displayed | label used here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FL_493` | 2 | broad | `QID1459`: "...advertise the snacks to women directly" | **targeted** |
| `FL_497` | 1 | female | `QID1460`: "...advertise the snacks broadly to the general public" | **broad** |

The contrast everywhere below is **mean(fair1 | broad shown) − mean(fair1 | targeted shown)**, defined by what was displayed.

> **Labelling note.** The embedded field `target` is the reverse of what each arm displays: the arm that records target='broad' (Segment=2) shows the vignette in which the company advertises 'to women directly', and the arm that records target='female' (Segment=1) shows the 'broadly to the general public' vignette. The original analysis conditions on `Segment` and does not use `target`, so its estimate is unaffected, but anyone who labels arms by `target` would reverse the sign of the effect. This is an instrument labelling issue read off the exposure traces, not an execution failure.

## 5. Planted-effect check (mock)

The mock adds ±1 to a seeded 3–7 rating according to which vignette text it can read, so the planted contrast is **+2** when the instrument's context reaches the model and **0** when it doesn't. Both conditions use the same simulator seed, so assignments and traces are identical; only the model's context differs.

| condition | estimate [95% CI] | recovers planted effect? | CI excludes 0? |
|---|---|---|---|
| mock | +1.81 [+1.52, +2.10] | yes | yes |
| isolated-mock | -0.19 [-0.48, +0.10] | no | no |

With the instrument preserved, the pipeline recovers the planted effect (+1.81). The isolated baseline, the *same* model asked `fair1` without the vignette, estimates -0.19: it cannot respond to a treatment it never sees, so a context-free pipeline reports a null effect even for a respondent model whose behaviour depends entirely on the treatment. Its execution gate still passes, because the *instrument* was administered correctly; the information-state table in section 1 is what exposes the difference. This is why the treatment screens, and not only the outcome questions, must reach the respondent model.
